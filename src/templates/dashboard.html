End-to-End Production Architecture

Designed according to leak-free ML engineering best practices.

1

Leak-Free Preprocessing & Scaler Isolation

Data split is strictly stratified 64/16/20 (Train / Validation / Test). The Amount StandardScaler is fitted solely on the training split and serialized to avoid test statistics leakage.

2

Champion Selection via PR-AUC & Registry Alias

Trained multiple models (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM). Model selection optimizes Precision-Recall AUC on the validation set. Tagged with alias 'production' in MLflow Registry.

3

Multi-Cloud Artifact Storage (Hugging Face Hub & AWS S3)

Heavy binary weights (.pkl) are stored outside Git. Scripts automatically generate Model Card (README.md) with verifiable metrics directly from MLflow runs.

4

High-Throughput Serving & Enterprise Monitoring

FastAPI async engine delivers sub-2.5ms inference. Continuous monitoring via Prometheus metrics (/metrics) and Grafana dashboards for latency and prediction drift.
